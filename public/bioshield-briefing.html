What it is

An editor's brief, every day.

Biosecurity intelligence is scattered across defence blogs, agencies, journals and wires. BioShield consolidates it into one daily briefing, an editorial view with a TL;DR up top, the lead story, and every other article tagged and contextualised.

It is more than a feed: each item is selected and framed by the Best Practice AI team, so you read what matters and skip what doesn't. The same evidence stream becomes the live input to the Resilience Index's PAT scoring engine.
