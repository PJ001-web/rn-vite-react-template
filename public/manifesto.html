Global Resilience Manifesto.

Published 20 September 2026By Resilience Nexus

Global resilience will define the next era of prosperity. It will determine which countries remain secure, which companies continue to create value, which institutions retain trust and which families carry opportunity forward. Building it is one of the most important, shared endeavours of our time.

Resilience is also becoming one of the world’s greatest economic and investment opportunities. The systems on which prosperity depends must be strengthened, renewed and built for a more turbulent world. The countries, companies and investors that act now will create lasting value while building the capabilities the future demands.

The Resilience Nexus is The Platform for global resilience. It gives countries, companies, institutions and families the means to see and strengthen their own resilience. It reveals where resilience is strong, where it is breaking down and where action and investment can make the greatest difference.

What global resilience is

A different question.

What determines whether countries, companies, institutions and families remain secure, continue to create value, retain trust and carry opportunity forward?

That is an economic and strategic question before it is an investment question.

Global resilience concerns the capacity to persevere, adapt and transform across economic, institutional, technological, geopolitical, environmental, societal and competitive dimensions. It applies across investment and capital allocation, strategy, leadership, operations, policy, procurement, supply chains, institutional reform, innovation or collaboration.

It starts with the ability of a country, company, institution or family to prosper through change. It applies during growth, stability, transition, innovation and renewal as much as during shocks or crises. A system does not need to be under threat before its resilience matters.

Global resilience is not the sum of individually resilient countries, companies, institutions and families. It arises from what they build, the choices they make and the dependencies through which their actions affect one another.
