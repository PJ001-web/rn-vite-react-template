What it is

See the dependency you forgot you had.

Most organisations know their tier-one suppliers and almost nothing beyond. We model the network downstream, and upstream, to find where many of your critical inputs quietly converge on a single source, region or chokepoint.

The output ties back to the Resilience Index: concentration and propagation risk become inputs to the Context and Convergence dimensions, so supply-chain fragility shows up in the same score the board already tracks.
