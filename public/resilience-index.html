What it is

An index, not a dashboard.

Most resilience tools hand you a wall of indicators and leave the judgement to you. The Resilience Index does the judgement, it resolves hundreds of signals into one score, on one scale, with a confidence interval attached.

It runs on our proprietary PAT framework, Persevere, Adapt, Transform, the methodology we own. Every new signal updates the score by a calibrated amount, and the framework is version-controlled and auditable. The result is comparable across nations, sectors and institutions, and stable enough to set strategy against.
