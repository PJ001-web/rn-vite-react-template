What it is

Before / after, on one scale.

A scenario is a defined shock applied to a profile. We model how it propagates through the six dimensions and read the resulting movement in the Persevere, Adapt and Transform scores, the before, the after, and the gap it opens.

Because every scenario runs on the same methodology as the Resilience Index, the results are directly comparable: you can rank shocks by impact, and rank profiles by how well they hold up.
