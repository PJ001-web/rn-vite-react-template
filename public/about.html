Our approach

Resilience, recognised as a core driver of success.

The Resilience Nexus exists to empower leaders, businesses and investors to harness the power of resilience, driving innovation, adaptability and sustainable growth.

The thesis

Beyond risk management.

We are building a world where resilience is recognised not just as a survival mechanism, but as a core driver of long-term success and transformation.

Traditional risk management is defensive, a premium paid against the next shock. Our work reframes resilience as an economic model: a strategic capability that strengthens competitive position, reduces the cost of disruption, and unlocks new opportunities for growth. The difference is not academic. It changes what you measure, what you fund, and who in the organisation owns it.

The framework

Three outcomes. Six dimensions. One Nexus.

A company or country that pursues all three resilience outcomes at the same time, understanding that each requires a different strategy, has entered the Resilience Nexus, and the strategic resilience space.

Persevere

Hold the line

Absorb the shock and protect the core without losing essential function.

Adapt

Bend, don't break

Reconfigure quickly as conditions shift, agility as a standing capability.

Transform

Emerge stronger

Use disruption to reinvent and capture the advantage others miss.

Each outcome is assessed across six dimensions, Context, Convergence, Navigation, Strategy, Outcomes and Impact, and resolved into a single score by the Resilience Index. See how the Index works →

The foundation

A framework, written down.

The Resilience Nexus methodology is grounded in Dr Kelly Culver's framework, set out in Strategic Resilience for Sustainable Impact.

Its second contribution, navigational leadership, names the capability that decides whether an organisation actually reaches the Nexus: the capacity to steer through ambiguity and convergence without a fixed map. It is the dimension most often missing, and the one the Index is built to surface. Read the white paper →

How we work

From diagnosis to durable advantage.

01

Diagnose

We score the profile on the Resilience Index, establishing where it sits relative to peers and which dimensions carry the most risk.

02

Stress-test

We run the relevant shocks as scenarios, exposing where one of the three forces is quietly carrying the others.

03

Strategise

We translate the score into the single biggest gap to close, and the investable moves to close it.

04

Track

The Index keeps updating as signals arrive, so resilience becomes a number you govern against over time.
